# 02. Problem Statement

## Introduction
Decision-making in modern enterprises is often reactive rather than proactive.

## Purpose
To define the core challenge that ForesightAI aims to resolve within the context of the AI/ML Hackathon track.

## Detailed Explanation
1. **Uncertainty**: Businesses struggle to accurately predict future outcomes based on historical data.
2. **The "Black Box" Problem**: Existing AI solutions provide predictions but no explanations, leading to a lack of trust from stakeholders.
3. **Lack of Actionability**: A prediction without a recommendation leaves decision-makers asking, "What do I do next?"
4. **Static Analysis**: Traditional dashboards show what happened, not what *will* happen if certain variables change.
