# 12. Business Model

## Introduction
ForesightAI is designed to deliver immediate ROI to enterprise clients by optimizing their decision-making processes.

## Purpose
To define the commercialization strategy for the hackathon presentation.

## Detailed Explanation
- **Target Users**: C-Level Executives, Operations Managers, Financial Analysts, Data Scientists.
- **Value Proposition**: Reduce risk, optimize resource allocation, and increase trust in AI through explainability.
- **Revenue Model**: B2B SaaS (Software as a Service). Tiered pricing based on prediction volume and feature access (e.g., Basic, Pro, Enterprise).
